The Founders

The Minds Behind
Re-Root

Founded by high schoolers with a vision for social entrepreneurship — built to serve as a bridge between tribal legacy and the 21st-century marketplace.

Naisha Palawat

Founder

Founder of Project Re-Root, Naisha brings a designer's eye and a researcher's rigour to cultural preservation work. She identified the need to make tribal art not just visible, but commercially viable — giving communities economic agency alongside cultural pride.

Yashkit Jain

Co-Founder

Yashkit built the operational framework that turned a documentation project into a scalable model for cultural and economic revival. His focus on market access and artisan partnerships forms the backbone of Re-Root's LIGHT methodology.

The Origin Story

From Documentation to Movement

Founded from a simple realization: tribal art belongs on the global stage, not just in history books. As high schoolers with a vision for social entrepreneurship, Naisha and Yashkit built Re-Root to serve as a bridge.

They identify regional communities with deep artistic legacies and provide the design intervention and market access needed to thrive in the 21st century. What started as a documentation project has evolved into a scalable model for cultural and economic revival.
