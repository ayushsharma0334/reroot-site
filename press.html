Presidential Recognition

Presented Before
President Murmu

Shahida Khanam was invited to present KTAS designs at Chashme Shahi before President Droupadi Murmu — bringing the Gujjar Bakarwal craft tradition to the highest level of national visibility.

This recognition reflects the significance of community-led cultural preservation and the growing national conversation around tribal heritage and sustainable craft economies.
