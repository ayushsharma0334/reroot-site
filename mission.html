About Project Re-Root

Reviving Tradition,
Reimagining Craft.

"Many cultural practices exist not in written form, but in what people make, wear, and use."

Project Re-Root creates platforms that bring visibility to these traditions while supporting artisans through skill development and global outreach.

By recording the memories of elders, reviving traditional embroidery and dress, and supporting women artisans with training and livelihood opportunities, the organization works to ensure that tribal knowledge continues to thrive.

Our vision is to build a living cultural archive where traditions are not only preserved but actively practiced and shared with future generations.

Our Vision

"A living cultural archive where traditions are not only preserved but actively practiced."

Through our museum, craft initiatives, and storytelling projects, we seek to celebrate identity, strengthen community pride, and sustain cultural heritage — connecting heritage with contemporary spaces so that living traditions are practiced, valued, and sustained.
